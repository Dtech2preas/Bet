Good to know

A few common questions.

Who is the portal for?

It is designed for small businesses and service providers who want one place for client records, products, quotes, invoices and payments. SaaS businesses can also organise software products, licences and agreements.

Can I start without paying?

Yes. The Free plan is R0 per month and includes capped client, product and monthly quote/invoice allowances. Paid features and higher limits are available on Pro and Business/Premium.

Does it connect directly to my bank?

No direct bank connection is advertised. The financial tools support importing bank statement CSV files; you can review suggested matches and categories before reconciling.

Does it file tax returns for me?

No. SARS/CIPC-oriented reports help organise financial information; they are not a tax filing service or a substitute for advice from a qualified tax or accounting professional.

Can I put my own brand on documents?

Pro and Business/Premium plans support custom logo and brand colours on PDFs. Custom PDF templates are listed as a Business/Premium feature.

How do I check current billing terms?

The portal offers monthly, three-month and yearly billing selections. Check the subscription page for the current price and terms for the billing interval you choose.
